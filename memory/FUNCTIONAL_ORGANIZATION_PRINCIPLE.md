# Functional Organization (FO) — Organizing Principle for Future Development

Adopted 2026-06 (Compass 3.0, Sprint 1.4). Guides future development of Functional
Organization (FO) and future revisions of Composition Management (CM). Not a schema change.

## Principle
Writing develops as learners organize communicative FUNCTIONS into coherent communication.
Students often produce writing that contains many worthwhile ideas. The developmental
challenge is not simply to improve individual sentences — it is to organize the communicative
work those ideas perform.

## Hidden reasoning to increase (messy / accumulative paragraphs)
When Compass encounters a messy paragraph, its hidden reasoning should increasingly ask:
1. What communicative function is each idea currently serving?
2. Which communicative functions are missing?
3. Which functions are present but disconnected?
4. How can the relationships among those functions become more coherent?

## Implication
Sentence organization, paragraph organization, and essay organization all EMERGE from this
deeper process of organizing communicative functions. FO and CM should be built on this
organizing lens rather than on surface sentence-level fixes.
